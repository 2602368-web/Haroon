Islamabad, The Beautiful

Islamabad, the federal capital of Pakistan,
is a master-planned city renowned for its green spaces, clean atmosphere, and peaceful lifestyle.
Nestled at the foothills of the Margalla Hills, the city was built during the 1960s to replace Karachi as the national capital. Its organized grid system is divided into well-structured sectors, wide tree-lined avenues, and expansive parks like Fatima Jinnah Park. Islamabad serves as the political and administrative heart of the country—housing the Parliament, the Supreme Court, and numerous foreign embassies—while also standing out for iconic architectural and cultural landmarks such as the Bedouin-tent-inspired Faisal Mosque and the multi-tiered Pakistan Monument.
